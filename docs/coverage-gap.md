# Coverage Gap Analysis

A map of where TME evidence exists and where it does not, with Indian
representation tracked separately.

Source of truth: `data/coverage.csv`. Figure: `figures/coverage-heatmap.png`,
generated by `scripts/make_heatmap.py`.

## Evidence codes

| Code | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `direct` | Direct measurement in patient tissue |
| `transcript` | Transcript-level proxy only |
| `inferred` | Inferred from a related measurement |
| `none` | No data found |
| `india: yes` | Indian cohort data exists for this combination |

## Current state

8 factors x 5 tumor types = 40 cells.

- **31 cells (78%) have no data**
- **5 cells (12%) have Indian data**
- **Cervical cancer: entirely empty** across all eight factors

## Where the Indian data sits

| Factor | Tumor type | Evidence | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hypoxia / HIF-1a | Oral/HNSCC | Direct | Multiple IHC studies, incl. Northeast India |
| Hypoxia / HIF-1a | Gallbladder | Inferred | HIF1 activation in iTRAQ proteomics |
| Collagen I | Gallbladder | Inferred | ECM remodelling in iTRAQ proteomics |
| Oestrogen / receptor | Breast | Direct | ICGA cohort, Indian BC transcriptomics |
| Lactate | Oral/HNSCC | Inferred | GLUT1/HIF-1a proxy, Indian OSCC and dysplasia |

Four of the five trace to two research groups: ICMR-NIP Delhi (gallbladder
proteomics) and Indian oral pathology departments (HIF IHC). The Indian evidence
base is narrower than a paper count suggests.

## Concrete gaps worth naming

1. **No Indian tumor stiffness measurement exists.** No AFM, rheometry, or
   elastography characterisation of Indian tumor tissue was found. Directly
   addressable with standard biomaterials-lab equipment.
2. **No Indian pH or IFP measurement exists.** Lactate has an Indian proxy signal
   only (GLUT1/HIF-1a in OSCC), not a concentration measurement.
3. **Cervical cancer is uncharacterised for every factor in this atlas**, despite
   high Indian incidence. Indian cervical genomic and expression datasets exist,
   so the transcript-level cells are fillable without new experiments.
4. **ICGA can test a specific published claim.** Byrne et al. found collagen I
   higher in ER+/PGR+ than triple-negative breast tumors. ICGA has an ER+/PR+
   Indian cohort with proteomics, in a population presenting at younger age.
   Whether that finding replicates is a well-posed, tractable question.

## Why this is the contribution

- No published map of TME evidence coverage exists, and none tracking Indian
  representation.
- It converts "Indian data is missing" from a limitation into the finding.
- It is directly actionable: it identifies which experiments have not been done.
- It fits on one slide.
- It is achievable by one person.

## Honest limitations

- One round of systematic searching, not a formal systematic review. Absence of
  evidence here means absence of *found* evidence.
- Search was English-language and database-indexed. Indian journal literature
  not indexed in PubMed may contain relevant work.
- 5 tumor types and 8 factors is a pilot grid. Target is 15 factors x 8 tumor
  types.
